## Q1 — Which candidate factor is associated with churn in this synthetic dataset?

### Answer

The first check did not meet its predeclared support threshold: monthly-plan churn was 7.5% and annual-plan churn was 9.6%, a difference of -2.0 percentage points (monthly minus annual; absolute difference 2.0 points). The hypothesis that the absolute difference is at least 5 points was not supported. This does not show that the plans are equivalent; the analysis is descriptive and has no uncertainty estimate.

The follow-up check found a higher observed churn rate among customers with at least two recent support cases (26.7%, n=251) than among customers with fewer cases (3.8%, n=949), a difference of 22.9 percentage points. The observed difference met the example's 10 percentage-point threshold. This is an association in generated data, not evidence that support cases cause churn.

### Confidence and limits

The arithmetic is reproducible from the checked-in CSV and script. The dataset is synthetic and encodes its own generation process. The group comparisons are unadjusted; they do not establish causality, statistical significance, or real-world customer behavior.
